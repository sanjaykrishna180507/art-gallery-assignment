The Starry Night

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year of Creation: 1889

Category: Post-Impressionism


Description: Painted from the asylum room of Van Gogh in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, this famous oil canvas depicts a sweeping night sky with swirling blue energy and glowing yellow stars over a quiet village.
